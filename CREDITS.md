All of them were treated the same way on the page: warm monochrome, with the
paper colour multiplied over them.

The garments themselves are not photographs. They are drawn in code as
parametric SVG in `src/components/Garment.tsx` — silhouette, marled flecks
of the four source dye batches, collar, seams and screen-printed mark — and
are the only colour on the site.
